About Mahamodul Hasan Moon

Mahamodul Hasan Moon is a full-stack developer, AI automation specialist and DevOps engineer from Dhaka, Bangladesh, and the founder of Brainicsoft Digital.

I have spent 6+ years building full stack web applications and running them reliably in production.

I work across three areas: full stack development with Next.js, Node.js and NestJS; AI automation that removes repetitive work from business workflows; and DevOps with Docker, GitHub Actions and AWS so every release is fast and safe.

In 2023 I founded Brainicsoft Digital. Through it, and through Upwork and Fiverr, I work with clients around the world.
